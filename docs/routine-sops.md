## SOP: Adding a new product to the website

For a new honey, candle, soap, lip balm or hamper. Do the Shopify part first, then the website tool.


### A. In Shopify

1. Products → Add product. Give it a title, a price, a main photo and its stock (turn on “Track quantity”).
2. In the sales channels box, make sure **Headless** is ticked. If it is not, the website cannot see the product.
3. Save it. Anything the website needs from Shopify (name, photo, web address) it reads for itself.

### B. On the website tool

1. Go to your site address followed by `/tools/new-product` and sign in with the Studio login.
2. Pick the product under **Shopify product**, then choose **What is it?**: Honey, or Candles, soap, lip balm or a hamper.
3. Fill in the boxes: **Name on the site**, **Tagline**, and the description (“Where it’s from (the story)” for honey, “Description” for everything else).
4. Honey also needs **Postcode area or district** (for example BS3), the **Beekeeper** and the **Jar size**. Other products need a **Category**, and an optional **Size**.
5. Enter **Packed weight in grams (as posted)**. That includes the packaging, and it is sent to Shopify for postage.
6. Tick **Show it on the site straight away** only when it is ready to sell. Leave **Soften the photo’s edges** off if the Shopify photo is already softened.
7. Press **Add to the site**, then follow the links it shows to look at the new page.

### C. In Studio afterwards

- **Soap and lip balm:** open the product and paste the **Ingredients** exactly from the approved label. Check the size shows what you intend.
- **Honey:** tick “Meets the Gert Lush Standard” only after the beekeeper review and batch record are done.
- **Honey:** once it is on, it also appears as a choice in “Choose your own” hampers. If two honeys share a postcode, the map shows both.
- **Subscriptions:** only add a subscription price after a real plan exists in Shopify’s Subscriptions app.

### If something goes wrong

- “The site can’t see that Shopify product” means Headless is not ticked on the product.
- “Add a photo to the product in Shopify first”: the tool copies the Shopify photo, so add one there.
- The page says pricing is unavailable: the web address (handle) in Studio does not match Shopify. Ask for help.

## SOP: Approving a customer review

Reviews never appear on the site until you approve them. This protects you from spam and abuse.


### When it happens

An email arrives at admin@ with the subject “New 4/5 review for Bee S3 — needs approving”. It shows the reviewer’s name, the rating and what they wrote.


### Steps

1. Open Studio and choose **Product Review**. Open the newest one (it says “awaiting approval”).
2. Read it. Approve it if it is a genuine opinion about the product.
3. Tick **Approved** and press **Publish**. It shows on that product page within about a minute and counts towards the star rating.

### Do not approve

- Spam, adverts or links.
- Rude or abusive comments, or anything naming another person.
- Phone numbers, addresses or other personal details.
- Health claims (for example that honey cured something).
Leave **Approved** unticked, or delete the review in Studio. If you are unsure, leave it unticked.


### Good to know

- You can leave a review unapproved indefinitely; nothing breaks.
- Reviewers do not give their email address, so you cannot reply to them.
- Approved reviews can also appear next to your products in search results, so only approve ones you are happy to stand behind.

## SOP: A hamper order with a problem

A hamper is its own product, and the website takes the honey out of stock for you when the order is paid. This is the case where it could not.


### When it happens

An email arrives at admin@ with the subject “Action needed: hamper order #… — stock not deducted”. It means the honey chosen for that hamper could not be trusted, so **no honey stock was deducted**. It gives the reason and what the customer’s order said.

This is rare. It happens if the jars chosen do not add up to the hamper size, or a honey named is not one you sell.


### Steps

1. In Shopify, open the order named in the email. Look at the hamper line and its **Honey selection**.
2. If the choice is clear and sensible (for example a 3-jar hamper with 2 of one honey and 1 of another), pack it as written.
3. If it is not clear, or the jars do not add up, email the customer to confirm what they want. If you need to, pack a sensible mix and tell them.
4. Take the honey out of stock by hand: Shopify → Products → Inventory, and lower each honey by the jars you packed.
5. Fulfil the order as usual.

### Good to know

- The customer has paid in full, so always fulfil it; this is only about stock and the choice of honey.
- A hamper only shows “Choose your own” honeys that are in stock, so overselling a single honey is unlikely.
