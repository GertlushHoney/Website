Guidelines.docx`, `Revised Commercial Model.docx`. Where the non-headless Project Pack and

## Explicitly not in this pass

Per `Gert Lush Honey Website Implementation.docx`: "Do not create generic finished homepage
designs during this task. Concentrate on a strong, documented and testable foundation." Full
page visual design begins once this foundation is reviewed and approved, and once Shopify/Sanity
accounts exist to build real integrations against (rather than guessing at a data shape that
would likely need rework).
